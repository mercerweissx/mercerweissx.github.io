## The Author 
I'm a hobbyist computer programmer based in Saint Louis, Missouri. I love to write Java programs, tinker with Linux, build things out of wood, spend time reading, write out my thoughts, and turn random questions that pop into my head into personal research projects. I created this site with the hope that someone else might be interested in some of my writings.

I hold a culturally-progressive and economically-conservative libertarian worldview, ensuring members of both major American political parties find me extremely irritating to talk to. While I don't intend to write on explicitly political topics, my political views will nonetheless influence how I write about the decidedly non-political.

<hr>

## The Content
This blog hosts my personal essays and research projects, largely consisting of either facts I found interesting enough to write about or my own musings on various subjects. I also intend to use this blog to showcase programming projects which I consider personal achievements.

Despite my political leanings, I remain extremely skeptical toward the use of generative artificial intelligence for almost all purposes. I write neither prose nor programs with the help of AI tooling, and have no intention of doing so in the future. Bite me, futurists.
