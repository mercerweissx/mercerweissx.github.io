# Introduction
Just over a year ago I decided I wanted a personal motto, something to focus my mind on to remind myself of my goals and values.
I wanted something distinct, but not esoteric. I wanted something in Latin (the language of learning), but not a tired platitude. After an
afternoon of gentle soul-searching and fierce googling, I settled on something I could call my own...

___Animus Supra Omnia.___

The originally intended meaning was "passion above all", but as I searched for a suitable Latin translation I was pleasantly surprised to find words which even better communicated my values through their shades of subtlety.

# Explication
## Animus...
The meaning of the word "passion" can be difficult to pin down [1]. While I *do* mean passion in the sense of "following your passion", I'm
trying to communicate something much deeper than enthusiasm. My passion is for thinking, learning, reasoning, 
studying, and understanding. Knowledge, and my reverence for it, guides my life in ways beyond mere preferences. True learning, the 
pure *satisfaction* of grasping something previously incomprehensible, is what I live for.

Enter the Latin word "animus". Both "animus" and "anima" have been given new meanings in English over the centuries, but here I'm only
discussing the original Latin terms. "Anima" is understood to mean something's actual soul or essence, its literal breath of life. "Animus",
on the other hand, is taken to mean the spiritual force that *causes* someone to move, the force guiding them. It's your mind, your
reason, your intention, your courage, your willpower. "Animus", as I mean it, is your fire of will, your truest *passion*.
