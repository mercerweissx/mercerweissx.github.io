# Introduction
Just over a year ago I decided I wanted a personal motto, something to focus my mind on to remind myself of my goals and values.
I wanted something distinct, but not esoteric. I wanted something in Latin (the language of learning), but not a tired platitude. After an
afternoon of gentle soul-searching and fierce googling, I settled on something I could call my own...

___Animus Supra Omnia.___

The originally intended meaning was "passion above all", but as I searched for a suitable Latin translation I was pleasantly surprised to find words which even better communicated my values through their shades of subtlety.
